## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### Brand Voice Attributes

- **Professional** - We're a serious tool for serious work
- **Helpful** - We guide, don't lecture
- **Clear** - No jargon, plain language
- **Confident** - We know our product delivers value
- **Friendly** - Approachable, not corporate

### Writing Style

**DO:**
- Use active voice ("Create a roadmap" not "A roadmap can be created")
- Be specific with numbers and features
- Focus on benefits, then features
- Use customer-centric language ("you" and "your")
- Keep sentences short and scannable

**DON'T:**
- Use excessive superlatives ("best", "amazing", "revolutionary")
- Make vague promises without backing them up
- Use technical jargon without explanation
- Write long, complex sentences
- Focus only on features without benefits

### Example Transformations

**Bad:** "Our revolutionary product provides an amazing way to facilitate customer engagement through a best-in-class feedback collection mechanism."

**Good:** "Product Roadmap lets customers vote on features and submit ideas, so you build what they actually want."
