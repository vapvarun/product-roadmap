# Product Overview Video Script

## Video Details
- **Title:** Product Roadmap for WordPress - Build Products Your Customers Love
- **Duration:** 2-3 minutes
- **Purpose:** YouTube/Website hero video, product introduction
- **Tone:** Professional, enthusiastic, clear
- **Target:** All audiences (first impression)

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:15)

**VISUAL:** Product logo animation, then roadmap board view

**NARRATOR:**
> "What if you could know exactly which features your customers want most? What if instead of guessing, you had real data to guide your product decisions?"

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Build Products Your Customers Love"

---

### THE PROBLEM (0:15 - 0:40)

**VISUAL:** Split screen showing scattered feedback (email, Slack, support tickets)

**NARRATOR:**
> "If you're building products, you probably know this struggle. Feature requests scattered across email, support tickets, and social media. Customers wondering if you're even listening. And you, trying to prioritize without real data."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Feature requests everywhere. No clear priorities."

**VISUAL:** Frustrated user leaving/churning animation

**NARRATOR:**
> "The result? Customers churn because they feel ignored. Teams build features nobody uses. And you're always playing catch-up."

---

### THE SOLUTION (0:40 - 1:00)

**VISUAL:** Product Roadmap board view appearing

**NARRATOR:**
> "Introducing Product Roadmap - a WordPress plugin that transforms how you collect customer feedback. Create beautiful public roadmaps where customers can vote on features and submit their own ideas."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Product Roadmap for WordPress"

**VISUAL:** Show voting in action (click, count increases)

**NARRATOR:**
> "Now your customers show you what they want - with real votes, not just likes. No more guessing. No more disconnect."

## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- Clean, modern aesthetic
- Smooth transitions between scenes
- Actual product screenshots, not mockups
- Minimal text overlays - let visuals speak
- WBcom Designs brand colors throughout

### Pacing
- Intro: Quick hook to grab attention
- Problem: Relatable, build empathy
- Solution: Relief, hope
- Features: Steady, informative
- CTA: Clear, actionable

### Music
- Upbeat but professional background track
- Lower during narrator segments
- Builds during CTA

### Screenshots Needed
- Board view (populated with demo items)
- Voting interaction (before/after)
- Suggestion form
- Comments section
- Status columns
- Analytics dashboard (Pro)
- Timeline view (Pro)
- Template gallery (Pro)
- Multi-product selector (Pro)

### Recording Options
1. **Screen recording** - Capture actual plugin usage
2. **Animated mockups** - Cleaner but less authentic
3. **Hybrid** - Real screenshots with smooth animations

### B-Roll Ideas
- Keyboard typing
- Mouse clicking
- Mobile device viewing roadmap
- Team looking at analytics
- Happy customer (stock footage)
