# Voting & Suggestions Feature Video Script

## Video Details
- **Title:** Product Roadmap Voting & Suggestions - Let Customers Shape Your Product
- **Duration:** 3-4 minutes
- **Purpose:** Feature deep-dive for voting and suggestions
- **Tone:** Informative, demonstrative
- **Target:** Users wanting to understand voting system in depth

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:20)

**VISUAL:** Roadmap with voting in action

**NARRATOR:**
> "Want to know what features your customers actually want? The voting and suggestions system in Product Roadmap gives you real data to guide your product decisions. In this video, I'll show you exactly how it works."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Voting & Suggestions Deep Dive"

---

### VOTING OVERVIEW (0:20 - 0:50)

**VISUAL:** Roadmap board with vote buttons visible

**NARRATOR:**
> "Let's start with voting. Every item on your roadmap has a vote button. Unlike simple 'like' buttons, this is real voting - customers can vote up for features they want, or vote down for ones they don't."

**VISUAL:** Show upvote and downvote arrows

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Real Votes, Not Just Likes"

**VISUAL:** Vote count on different items

> "The vote count shows you at a glance which features are most wanted. Sort by votes and your priorities become crystal clear."

---

### HOW USERS VOTE (0:50 - 1:30)

**VISUAL:** User perspective on roadmap page

**NARRATOR:**
> "From your customer's perspective, voting is simple. They see a feature, click the upvote arrow, and their vote is counted immediately."

**VISUAL:** Click upvote, count increases

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "One Click to Vote"

**VISUAL:** Arrow turns green/highlighted

> "The arrow changes color to show they've voted. If they change their mind, they can click again to remove their vote, or click the down arrow to switch."

**VISUAL:** Show vote removal and switching

**VISUAL:** Single item page with StackOverflow-style voting

> "On the item detail page, voting is even more prominent - with large up and down arrows in a StackOverflow-style layout."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "StackOverflow-Style Voting"

---

### GUEST VOTING (1:30 - 2:00)

**VISUAL:** Logged out user on roadmap

**NARRATOR:**
> "Here's something important: guest voting. By default, users don't need an account to vote. This removes friction and dramatically increases participation."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Guest Voting - No Account Required"

**VISUAL:** Guest voting in action

> "We track votes by IP address to prevent gaming the system. One vote per person, without requiring registration."

**VISUAL:** Settings page showing guest voting option

> "You can change this in settings if you want to require login, but I recommend keeping guest voting enabled for maximum engagement."

---

### USER SUGGESTIONS (2:00 - 2:50)

**VISUAL:** Suggestion button/form on roadmap page

**NARRATOR:**
> "Now let's look at suggestions. This feature lets customers submit their own feature ideas, not just vote on yours."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "User Suggestions - Capture Every Idea"

**VISUAL:** Click 'Suggest Feature' button

> "On your roadmap page, there's a 'Suggest Feature' button. Clicking it opens a simple form."

**VISUAL:** Suggestion form

> "Customers enter a title, description, and optionally select a category. Then they submit."

**VISUAL:** Submit and see success message

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Simple Form, Powerful Results"

**VISUAL:** Admin notification or pending items list

> "The suggestion goes into a 'Pending' status for your review. Nothing goes public without your approval."

---

### MANAGING SUGGESTIONS (2:50 - 3:30)

**VISUAL:** WordPress Admin → Roadmap → All Items

**NARRATOR:**
> "In your admin dashboard, you'll see pending suggestions waiting for review."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Manage Suggestions"

**VISUAL:** Filter by pending status

> "Filter by 'Pending' to see all suggestions that need your attention."

**VISUAL:** Quick edit or edit a suggestion

> "Click to review the details. You can edit the content if needed, change the status to 'Planned' to approve it, or move it to trash if it's not something you'll pursue."

**VISUAL:** Approve and show on frontend

> "Once approved, the suggestion becomes a regular roadmap item that other customers can vote on."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Suggestion → Roadmap Item"

**VISUAL:** Settings - auto-approval option

> "If you trust your community, you can enable auto-approval in settings. But most people prefer the review workflow."

---

### TIPS & BEST PRACTICES (3:30 - 3:50)

**VISUAL:** Tips list with icons

**NARRATOR:**
> "A few quick tips:"

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Pro Tips"

> "One: Enable guest voting for maximum participation."

> "Two: Check suggestions regularly - customers appreciate quick responses."

> "Three: When you approve a popular suggestion, consider leaving a comment thanking the person who submitted it."

> "Four: Sort by votes when planning your roadmap - let the data guide you."

---

### CALL TO ACTION (3:50 - 4:00)

**VISUAL:** Roadmap with active voting

**NARRATOR:**
> "Voting and suggestions transform how you collect customer feedback. Try it on your roadmap and watch the insights roll in."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Start Collecting Feedback Today"

**VISUAL:** Logo + links

> "Check the description for links to our full documentation and more tutorials."

## Production Notes

### Key Demonstrations
1. Voting flow from user perspective
2. Vote count updating in real-time
3. Guest voting without login
4. Suggestion submission complete flow
5. Admin approval workflow
6. StackOverflow-style single item voting

### Screenshots/Recordings Needed
- Roadmap with vote buttons visible
- Upvote click animation
- Downvote click animation
- Single item page with voting panel
- Guest user view
- Suggestion form
- Success message after submission
- Admin pending items list
- Quick approve action
- Auto-approval setting

### Chapters
```
0:00 Introduction
0:20 How Voting Works
0:50 Voting from User's Perspective
1:30 Guest Voting Explained
2:00 User Suggestions Feature
2:50 Managing Suggestions in Admin
3:30 Tips & Best Practices
3:50 Wrap Up
```

### Common Questions to Address
- "Can users vote multiple times?" → No, tracked by IP/user
- "Can I disable downvoting?" → Not currently, but rare in practice
- "Can guests submit suggestions?" → Pro feature for guest suggestions
- "Are suggestions moderated?" → Yes, pending by default
