# Multi-Product Management Video Script (Pro Feature)

## Video Details
- **Title:** Product Roadmap Pro - Managing Multiple Products & Roadmaps
- **Duration:** 3-4 minutes
- **Purpose:** Deep-dive into multi-product feature for agencies and companies with multiple products
- **Tone:** Professional, practical, solution-focused
- **Target:** Agencies, SaaS with multiple products, enterprise users

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:20)

**VISUAL:** Multiple roadmap boards side by side

**NARRATOR:**
> "Managing multiple products? Running an agency with multiple clients? The Pro multi-product feature lets you manage separate roadmaps for each, all from one WordPress dashboard. Let me show you how it works."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Multi-Product: One Dashboard, Many Roadmaps"

---

### USE CASES (0:20 - 0:45)

**VISUAL:** Icons for different use cases

**NARRATOR:**
> "Multi-product support works for many scenarios."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Who Needs Multi-Product?"

**VISUAL:** SaaS icon

> "SaaS companies with multiple products - maybe a CRM, an email tool, and an analytics platform."

**VISUAL:** Agency icon

> "Agencies managing roadmaps for different clients."

**VISUAL:** Developer icon

> "Plugin developers with free and pro versions, or multiple add-ons."

**VISUAL:** Enterprise icon

> "Enterprise teams with different departments or business units."

---

### CREATING PRODUCTS (0:45 - 1:30)

**VISUAL:** WordPress Admin → Roadmap → Products

**NARRATOR:**
> "Let's set it up. Go to Roadmap, then Products in your admin."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Creating Products"

**VISUAL:** Product list (taxonomy view)

> "This is where you manage your products. It works like WordPress categories."

**VISUAL:** Add New Product form

> "Click Add New Product. Give it a name - this is what appears in filters and shortcodes. I'll create 'Mobile App' as an example."

**VISUAL:** Enter name, slug auto-generates

> "The slug is generated automatically - you'll use this in shortcodes."

**VISUAL:** Add product, appears in list

> "Click Add, and your product is ready."

**VISUAL:** Create another product

> "Let me create a second one called 'Web Platform' so we can see multi-product in action."

---

### ASSIGNING ITEMS (1:30 - 2:00)

**VISUAL:** Edit a roadmap item

**NARRATOR:**
> "Now let's assign items to products. Edit any roadmap item."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Assigning Items to Products"

**VISUAL:** Product meta box in sidebar

> "In the sidebar, you'll see a Products box. Check the product this item belongs to."

**VISUAL:** Check 'Mobile App'

> "I'll assign this one to Mobile App."

**VISUAL:** Save and show in admin list

> "When you view all items, you'll see a Product column showing which product each item belongs to."

**VISUAL:** Product filter dropdown in admin

> "Use the product filter to quickly find items for a specific product."

---

### DISPLAYING PRODUCTS (2:00 - 2:40)

**VISUAL:** Page editor with shortcode

**NARRATOR:**
> "Now let's display product-specific roadmaps. Create a new page for each product."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Displaying Product Roadmaps"

**VISUAL:** Type shortcode with product parameter

> "Use the shortcode with a product parameter: [roadmap product='mobile-app']"

**VISUAL:** Preview showing only Mobile App items

> "Now this page only shows items assigned to Mobile App."

**VISUAL:** Another page with 'web-platform'

> "Create another page with product='web-platform' for the Web Platform roadmap."

**VISUAL:** Show both pages side by side

> "Each product has its own dedicated roadmap page."

**VISUAL:** Shortcode with multiple products

> "You can even show multiple products together: product='mobile-app,web-platform' displays both."

---

### Common Questions
- "Is there a limit to products?" → Configurable, default 10
- "Can one item belong to multiple products?" → Yes
- "Can I move items between products?" → Yes, just edit and change
- "Do products have separate analytics?" → Yes, filterable
