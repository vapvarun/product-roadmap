# Timeline & Analytics Video Script (Pro Features)

## Video Details
- **Title:** Product Roadmap Pro - Timeline View & Analytics Dashboard
- **Duration:** 4-5 minutes
- **Purpose:** Showcase Pro-only features for potential upgraders
- **Tone:** Professional, demonstrative, value-focused
- **Target:** Free users considering Pro, new Pro users

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:20)

**VISUAL:** Pro badge, then timeline view

**NARRATOR:**
> "Ready to take your roadmap to the next level? In this video, I'll show you two powerful Pro features: the timeline view for visual planning and the analytics dashboard for data-driven decisions."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Pro Features: Timeline & Analytics"

---

### TIMELINE OVERVIEW (0:20 - 0:50)

**VISUAL:** Full timeline view with items

**NARRATOR:**
> "The timeline view transforms your roadmap into a visual Gantt-style chart. Instead of columns, you see items laid out across time, making it perfect for project planning and client presentations."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Timeline View - Visual Project Planning"

**VISUAL:** Pan across timeline showing items

> "Each item appears as a bar, color-coded by status. You can see at a glance what's planned, what's in progress, and what's coming up."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Quarter View"

**VISUAL:** Switch to Year view

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Year View"

**VISUAL:** Navigate between periods

---

**VISUAL:** Assignee avatars

> "Assignee avatars show who's working on what - great for team coordination."

### Key Demonstrations
1. Timeline view with populated data
2. All period views (day, week, month, quarter, year)
3. Timeline navigation (previous/next)
4. Dependencies and progress bars
5. Full analytics dashboard
6. Both charts with interactivity
7. Both tables with clickable items
8. Filtering by product and time
9. CSV export
