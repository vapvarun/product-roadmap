## PRICING OBJECTIONS

### "It's too expensive"

**Understand:** What price point would work for you?

**Reframe:** Let's calculate the value:
- How many hours/week do you spend organizing feedback?
- What's your effective hourly rate?
- If Pro saves 2 hours/week at $50/hour, that's $400/month value
- Pro pays for itself in about [X] weeks

**Compare:**
- vs. SaaS alternatives ($50-100+/month) = $600-1200+/year
- vs. custom development = $10,000+
- Pro is one-time, not recurring

**Options:**
- Start with the free version
- Upgrade when you need analytics/timeline/multi-product

---

### "I don't want to pay for software"

**Acknowledge:** Totally understand. That's why we have a full-featured free version.

**Clarify:** The free version includes:
- Public roadmap display
- Full voting system
- User suggestions with workflow
- Comments & discussion
- Changelog feature
- 2 templates

**Explain:** Pro is for when you need:
- Analytics dashboards
- Timeline/Gantt views
- Multiple products
- Advanced features

Many people use Free indefinitely.

---

### "Why is Pro a one-time purchase?"

**Explain:** We believe in fair pricing:
- You pay once, use forever
- All updates included
- No subscription fatigue
- You own your data (it's on your server)

**Compare:** Monthly subscriptions add up:
- $50/month = $600/year = $1,800 over 3 years
- One-time purchase is better long-term value

## FEATURE OBJECTIONS

### "The free version is enough for me"

**Agree:** It might be! We built Free to be genuinely useful.

**Plant seeds:** Keep Pro in mind if you ever:
- Need to present roadmap data to stakeholders
- Want visual timeline views for planning
- Manage multiple products or clients
- Spend time on manual reporting

**No pressure:** Use Free as long as it works. We're here when you're ready for more.

---

### "I don't need analytics"

**Understand:** How do you currently report on roadmap engagement?

**Reframe:** Analytics helps with:
- Stakeholder presentations ("Feature X has 47 votes")
- Trend identification ("Votes are up 30% this quarter")
- Priority justification ("Here's why we're building X")
- CSV export for external reporting

**Question:** Have you ever needed to justify a product decision? Analytics gives you the evidence.

---

### "Timeline view seems unnecessary"

**Understand:** What do your planning/review meetings look like?

**Use cases:**
- Client presentations (agencies love this)
- Sprint planning with visual timeline
- Stakeholder reviews (executives love Gantt charts)
- Release planning with target dates

**Alternative:** If you only need board view, Free has you covered.

---

### "I only have one product"

**Acknowledge:** Multi-product is definitely for specific use cases.

**Consider:**
- Do you have separate free/pro versions?
- Different product lines?
- Client projects (if agency)?
- Future products planned?

**If truly single product:** Free or Pro without multi-product is still valuable for analytics and timeline.

## COMPETITIVE OBJECTIONS

### "I'm already using [Canny/Productboard/etc.]"

**Understand:** What do you like about it? Any pain points?

**Differentiate:**
- **Pricing:** One-time vs. monthly subscription
- **Data ownership:** Your server vs. their server
- **WordPress native:** Seamless integration
- **Customization:** Full code access

**Question:** What's your annual cost? Calculate 3-year total and compare.

---

### "GitHub Issues works for us"

**Understand:** That can work, especially for technical users.

**Consider:**
- Are your end users comfortable with GitHub?
- Do non-developers submit feature requests?
- Do you have actual voting (not just reactions)?
- Is it public-friendly?

**Position:** Product Roadmap is for customer-facing roadmaps. GitHub is for developer-facing issues.

---

### "We'll build it ourselves"

**Respect:** That's definitely an option. Let me share some considerations:

**Time investment:**
- Design: 20+ hours
- Development: 80+ hours
- Testing: 20+ hours
- Maintenance: ongoing

**Cost estimate:** $10,000+ at standard rates

**Opportunity cost:** What else could your team build with that time?

**Alternative:** Start with Product Roadmap (free), customize if needed with our API and hooks.

---

### "We use Trello for this"

**Understand:** Trello is great for internal planning.

**Consider:**
- Is it public-facing for customers?
- Do you have native voting (not power-ups)?
- Can customers submit ideas easily?
- Do you have analytics on engagement?

**Position:** Product Roadmap is designed for public feedback collection. Trello is designed for team collaboration.

## RESPONSE FRAMEWORK

For any objection, follow this pattern:

1. **Listen** - Understand the real concern
2. **Acknowledge** - Validate their perspective
3. **Clarify** - Ask questions if needed
4. **Address** - Provide relevant information
5. **Confirm** - Check if concern is resolved
6. **Advance** - Move conversation forward

**Example:**

> **Objection:** "It's too expensive"
>
> **Listen:** *pause, don't interrupt*
>
> **Acknowledge:** "Budget is definitely important, especially for tools."
>
> **Clarify:** "When you say expensive, are you comparing to something specific? Or is it more about overall budget?"
>
> **Address:** *ROI calculation, comparison to alternatives, free version option*
>
> **Confirm:** "Does that help address the cost concern?"
>
> **Advance:** "Would you like to start with the free version and see the value firsthand?"
