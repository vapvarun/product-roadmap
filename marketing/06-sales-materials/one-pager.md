## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Dimensions
- US Letter: 8.5" x 11" (216 x 279 mm)
- A4: 210 x 297 mm
- Create both versions

### Colors
- Use WBcom Designs brand colors
- High contrast for readability
- Minimal color palette (3-4 colors)

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold sans-serif, 18-24pt
- Body: Regular sans-serif, 10-12pt
- Minimum font size: 8pt

### Icons
- Simple, recognizable icons
- Consistent style throughout
- 24x24 or 32x32 px at print resolution

### Print Considerations
- 300 DPI minimum
- CMYK color mode
- 0.125" bleed
- Safe margins: 0.25" from edge
