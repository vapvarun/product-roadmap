## Comparison Talking Points

### When Prospects Ask About Free

**Emphasize:**
- Complete, not crippled
- Full voting and suggestion system
- Production-ready for single products
- Can always upgrade later

**Avoid:**
- Pressuring to buy Pro
- Implying Free is limited

### When Prospects Ask About Pro

**Emphasize:**
- Specific features they need
- ROI (time saved, better decisions)
- One-time purchase value
- Seamless upgrade from Free

**Avoid:**
- Feature dumping
- Unnecessary features for their use case

### Handling "Why Should I Pay?"

**Response framework:**
1. Acknowledge Free is sufficient for basics
2. Ask about their specific needs (analytics? multiple products?)
3. Connect Pro features to their needs
4. Emphasize one-time purchase, not subscription
5. Mention time savings and better decisions

### Handling "Is Free Really Free?"

**Response framework:**
1. Yes, 100% free forever
2. No hidden paywalls or surprise limits
3. Pro is for additional features, not unlocking basics
4. We succeed when you succeed with our tools
