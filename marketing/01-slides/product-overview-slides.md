## Slide Design Notes

### Color Palette
Follow WBcom Designs brand colors

### Fonts
- Headlines: Bold sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins)
- Body: Regular sans-serif
- Size: Large enough to read from distance

### Images
- Use actual screenshots from the plugin
- Refer to screenshot-requirements.md for specific shots needed
- Add subtle shadows/borders to screenshots

### Layout Tips
- One main idea per slide
- Limit text - slides support speech, not replace it
- Use consistent placement for headlines
- Leave breathing room (white space)

### Animation
- Keep animations subtle
- Consistent transitions between slides
- Avoid distracting effects
