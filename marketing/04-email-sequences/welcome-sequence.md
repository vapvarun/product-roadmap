# Welcome Email Sequence

## Overview
5-email sequence for new Product Roadmap users (free version downloads)

**Goal:** Help users succeed with the plugin, introduce Pro features
**Timing:** Over 14 days
**Trigger:** Plugin download from WordPress.org or website

---

## EMAIL 1: Welcome
**Send:** Immediately after download
**Subject Lines (A/B test):**
- "Welcome to Product Roadmap - Let's get you started"
- "You're in! Here's how to set up your roadmap in 5 minutes"
- "Your Product Roadmap journey starts here"

### Content

---

## EMAIL 2: Feature Highlight - Voting
**Send:** Day 2
**Subject Lines:**
- "The voting feature your customers will love"
- "See what customers actually want (real data, not guesses)"
- "Your first votes are waiting..."

### Content

---

**Subject:** The voting feature your customers will love

Hi {first_name},

How's your roadmap coming along? I wanted to share some tips on the feature that makes Product Roadmap special: **voting**.

**Why voting matters:**

Instead of guessing what customers want, you let them show you. Real upvotes and downvotes on features give you data for prioritization.

**3 tips for more votes:**

1. **Keep guest voting enabled** - No account required = more participation
2. **Share your roadmap link everywhere** - Email signature, support replies, social media
3. **Respond to voted items** - When you start building a popular feature, comment to let people know

**Pro tip:** Sort your roadmap by "Most Voted" to instantly see your customer priorities.

[Button: See Your Roadmap]

Still setting up? Our [quick start guide](link) gets you running in 5 minutes.

## EMAIL 3: Feature Highlight - Suggestions
**Send:** Day 5
**Subject Lines:**
- "Capture ideas you'd never think of"
- "Your customers have ideas - here's how to collect them"
- "The feature request system that actually works"

### Content

---

**Subject:** Capture ideas you'd never think of

Hi {first_name},

Voting is great, but sometimes the best ideas come from customers themselves. That's where **User Suggestions** comes in.

**How it works:**

1. Customers click "Suggest Feature" on your roadmap
2. They submit a title and description
3. You review it in WordPress admin
4. Approve it and it becomes a voteable roadmap item

**You stay in control:** Nothing goes public without your approval. You can edit, improve, or decline suggestions.

**Real results:** One of our users discovered their most-requested feature through a customer suggestion - something they never would have thought to build.

[Button: Enable Suggestions]

Have questions? Just reply to this email.

{signature}

## SEQUENCE METADATA

### Segmentation
- Segment by: Download source, engagement (opens/clicks)
- Exit conditions: Purchases Pro, unsubscribes
- Move to upgrade sequence if: High engagement + Day 14

### Tracking
- Open rates by email
- Click-through rates
- Replies
- Conversion to Pro

### A/B Testing Priority
1. Subject lines (all emails)
2. CTA button text
3. Email length (Email 4)
4. Pro mention timing (Email 4 vs Email 5)
