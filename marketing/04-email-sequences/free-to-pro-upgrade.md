# Free-to-Pro Upgrade Email Sequence

## Overview
3-email sequence targeting engaged free users who may benefit from Pro features

**Goal:** Convert engaged free users to Pro customers
**Timing:** After welcome sequence, triggered by engagement signals
**Trigger:** High engagement with free version OR browsed Pro page OR clicked Pro links

---

## TRIGGER CRITERIA

Send this sequence to users who have:
- Completed welcome sequence (14+ days)
- High email engagement (opened 3+ emails)
- Clicked Pro feature links
- Visited Pro landing page
- Have active roadmap with 10+ items
- Receiving significant votes (indicates real usage)

## POST-SEQUENCE ACTIONS

### If Purchased
- Move to Pro onboarding sequence
- Remove from upgrade sequences
- Tag as customer

### If No Purchase
- Move to general newsletter
- Suppress upgrade emails for 60 days
- Re-evaluate with future triggers (e.g., browsed Pro page again)

### If Unsubscribed
- Honor immediately
- Tag for suppression
- Consider feedback survey

---

## SEQUENCE METADATA

### Timing
- Email 1: Trigger + 0 days
- Email 2: Trigger + 3 days
- Email 3: Trigger + 7 days

### Exit Conditions
- Purchases Pro → exit, move to onboarding
- Unsubscribes → exit, suppress
- Completes sequence → move to newsletter, cool-off

### Tracking
- Open rates
- Click-through rates
- Conversion to Pro
- Revenue per email
- Time to purchase

### A/B Testing
1. Subject lines (urgency vs curiosity)
2. Email 1 focus (analytics vs timeline vs multi-product)
3. Email 3 approach (question vs offer)
4. Sequence timing (3-day gaps vs 5-day gaps)
