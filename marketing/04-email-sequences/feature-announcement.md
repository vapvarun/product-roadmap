# Feature Announcement Email Templates

## Overview
Templates for announcing new features, updates, and releases

**Goal:** Inform users, drive engagement with new features, showcase ongoing development
**Timing:** As features are released
**Audience:** All users (free), Pro users (Pro features), or segmented

---

## TEMPLATE 1: Major Feature Release

**Use for:** Significant new features, major version updates

### Subject Lines
- "New: [Feature Name] is here"
- "[Feature Name] just launched - see what you can do"
- "Big update: Introducing [Feature Name]"

### Content Template

---

**Subject:** New: [Feature Name] is here

Hi {first_name},

We just shipped something we're really excited about: **[Feature Name]**.

**What it does:**
[2-3 sentences explaining the feature and its benefit]

**Why you'll love it:**
- [Benefit 1]
- [Benefit 2]
- [Benefit 3]

[Screenshot or GIF of feature in action]

**How to use it:**
[Brief instructions or link to documentation]

[Button: Try [Feature Name] Now]

This feature is available in [Free version / Pro addon] version [X.X.X]. Update your plugin to get it.

Questions? Reply to this email or check our [documentation](link).

{signature}

P.S. - [Optional additional note, related feature, or Pro upsell for free users]

---

### Example: Changelog Feature

---

**Subject:** New: Changelog feature is here

Hi {first_name},

We just shipped something we're really excited about: **the Changelog feature**.

**What it does:**
Document your product releases right alongside your roadmap. Show customers what you've shipped, not just what you're planning.

**Why you'll love it:**
- Build trust by showing you deliver on roadmap items
- Create a public record of your progress
- Connect shipped features to original customer requests

[Screenshot of changelog view]

**How to use it:**
1. Go to Roadmap → Changelog → Add New
2. Add your version number and release notes
3. Display with `[roadmap_changelog]` shortcode

[Button: Create Your First Changelog Entry]

This feature is available in the free version (v1.3.0+). Update your plugin to get it.

Happy releasing!

{signature}

## SENDING GUIDELINES

### Segmentation
| Update Type | Audience |
|-------------|----------|
| Major Free feature | All users |
| Pro feature | Pro users (full), Free users (awareness) |
| Bug fix | All users |
| Beta | Engaged users, opted-in |

### Timing
- Major features: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm recipient time
- Bug fixes: Any time (brief)
- Milestones: Consider blog post + email

### Frequency
- Don't send more than 2 feature announcements per month
- Bundle small improvements into periodic updates
- Major features warrant standalone emails

### Follow-up
- Track engagement with new features
- Send tutorial content if feature is complex
- Survey users about feature satisfaction
