## FAQ SECTION

### Section Header
**Frequently Asked Questions**

### Q: Is it really free?
**A:** Yes! The free version is complete and fully functional. It's not a limited demo or trial. Use it forever at no cost.

### Q: Do I need coding skills?
**A:** Not at all. The setup wizard guides you through everything. Add your roadmap to any page with a simple shortcode.

### Q: Can visitors vote without creating an account?
**A:** Yes! Guest voting is enabled by default. We track by IP address to prevent duplicate votes.

### Q: How do I moderate suggestions?
**A:** All suggestions go into "Pending" status by default. Review them in your WordPress admin and approve or reject as needed.

### Q: Does it work with my theme?
**A:** Yes. Product Roadmap works with any WordPress theme. The design is responsive and adapts to your site's styling.

### Q: What if I want more features later?
**A:** Upgrade to the Pro addon anytime. It adds analytics, timeline views, templates, and more. Your existing data stays intact.

### Q: Is there a limit to items or votes?
**A:** No limits. Add as many roadmap items as you need, and there's no cap on votes.

### Q: Can I customize the appearance?
**A:** Yes. Custom CSS can adjust styling, and developers can override templates. Pro adds more template options.
