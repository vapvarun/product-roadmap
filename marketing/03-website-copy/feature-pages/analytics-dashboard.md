## CHARTS SECTION

### Section Header
**Visualize Your Data**

### Status Distribution Chart

A doughnut chart shows your items broken down by status:
- How many items are Planned?
- Are items getting stuck In Progress?
- What percentage is Complete?

**Why it matters:** Unbalanced distributions reveal bottlenecks. Too many items in one status? You might need to reprioritize.

**Interactive:** Click legend items to show/hide statuses.

---

### Voting Trends Chart

A line chart shows voting activity over time:
- Daily vote counts for selected period
- Spot engagement spikes
- Identify quiet periods
- Track growth trends

**Why it matters:** Voting trends tell you when customers are engaged. Launch something new? Watch for vote spikes. Quiet period? Maybe time to promote your roadmap.

**Interactive:** Hover for exact counts per day.

## FAQ SECTION

### Q: Is the dashboard real-time?
**A:** Dashboard shows data as of page load. Click Refresh to update without reloading the page.

### Q: How far back does data go?
**A:** All data from plugin installation. There's no automatic cleanup or data expiration.

### Q: Can I customize which metrics are shown?
**A:** Currently the dashboard shows fixed metrics. Custom dashboards may come in future versions.

### Q: Does it track individual user behavior?
**A:** No. Analytics shows aggregate data. Individual votes are anonymous for privacy.

### Q: Can I embed analytics on my site?
**A:** The dashboard is admin-only. For public metrics, consider exporting and displaying manually.

### Q: What format is the CSV export?
**A:** Standard CSV compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and business intelligence tools.
