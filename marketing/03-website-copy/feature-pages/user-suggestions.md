## OVERVIEW SECTION

### Content
Your customers have ideas you'd never think of. They use your product every day, encounter edge cases, and see opportunities you miss.

But how do you capture those ideas? Email gets lost. Support tickets mix bugs with features. Social media is scattered.

Product Roadmap's suggestion system creates a direct channel for customer ideas. They submit, you review, and the best ideas become roadmap items that everyone can vote on.

## HOW IT WORKS SECTION

### Section Header
**From Idea to Roadmap**

### The Customer Experience

**1. Click "Suggest Feature"**
A button on your roadmap page opens the submission form.

**2. Describe the Idea**
Simple form: title, description, optional category. No friction.

**3. Submit**
One click and it's sent. They see a confirmation message.

**4. Track Progress**
If approved, they can see their idea on the roadmap and watch votes come in.

### The Admin Experience

**1. Review Pending Items**
Suggestions appear in "Pending" status in your WordPress admin.

**2. Evaluate**
Read the suggestion. Is it feasible? Aligned with your vision?

**3. Take Action**
- **Approve**: Changes to "Under Review" or custom status, appears on public roadmap
- **Edit**: Improve the description before approving
- **Reject**: Move to trash (optionally notify submitter)

**4. Watch It Grow**
Approved suggestions become regular roadmap items. Other customers can vote and comment.

## FEATURE DETAILS SECTION

### Simple Submission Form

The suggestion form is intentionally simple:
- **Title** - Feature name (required)
- **Description** - What and why (required)
- **Category** - Optional classification

We don't ask for priority, estimated effort, or other complexity. Customers give you the idea; you handle the details.

---

### Pending Review Workflow

**Nothing goes public without your approval.**

All suggestions start in "Pending" status:
- Visible only to admins
- Listed in dedicated admin section
- Quick approve/reject actions
- Full edit capability

This keeps your public roadmap clean and professional while ensuring you never miss valuable input.

---

### Auto-Approval Option

For communities you trust, enable auto-approval:
- Suggestions go directly to "Under Review" status
- Appears on public roadmap immediately
- You can still edit or remove if needed
- Best for internal teams or engaged communities

---

### Guest Suggestions (Pro)

The Pro addon adds guest suggestions:
- Non-logged visitors can submit ideas
- Collect name and email
- Rate limiting prevents spam
- Email notifications for submitter
- Full moderation workflow

---

### Category Assignment

Suggestions can include a category:
- Pre-defined categories in dropdown
- Helps with organization
- Optional for submitters
- Admins can change during review

## WORKFLOW EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Approve and Celebrate

1. Customer submits: "Add dark mode"
2. You review: Great idea, highly requested
3. You approve with "Planned" status
4. You comment: "Great suggestion! We're adding this to Q2 plans."
5. Customer sees their idea with your acknowledgment
6. Other customers vote, building excitement

### Example 2: Edit and Improve

1. Customer submits: "Make it faster"
2. You review: Vague, but there's a real need
3. You edit title to: "Improve load time on dashboard"
4. You add details to description
5. Approve to "Under Review"
6. Now it's clear what's being considered

### Example 3: Reject Gracefully

1. Customer submits: "Add feature that conflicts with our vision"
2. You review: Not aligned with product direction
3. You trash the suggestion
4. Optionally, comment before rejecting explaining why
5. Customer appreciates the response even if answer is no

## BEST PRACTICES

### Respond Quickly
Check pending suggestions regularly. Quick responses (even rejections) build trust.

### Acknowledge Contributors
When approving, leave a comment thanking the person. They'll become advocates.

### Merge Duplicates
Similar suggestions? Approve one and note that it covers multiple requests.

### Track Sources
Note in comments if a suggestion came from a specific customer or context.

### Set Expectations
Your roadmap page should clarify that suggestions are reviewed, not guaranteed.

## FAQ SECTION

### Q: Are suggestions moderated by default?
**A:** Yes. All suggestions start in "Pending" status and require admin approval before appearing publicly.

### Q: Can I require login to submit suggestions?
**A:** In the free version, yes - suggestions require logged-in users. The Pro addon adds guest suggestions for non-logged visitors.

### Q: What happens if I reject a suggestion?
**A:** It moves to trash. You can optionally leave a comment before rejecting to explain why, which the submitter would see.

### Q: Can customers edit their suggestions after submitting?
**A:** No. Once submitted, only admins can edit. This prevents confusion if others have already commented.

### Q: How do I handle duplicate suggestions?
**A:** Approve the best-written one and trash duplicates. Consider mentioning in comments that multiple people requested this.

### Q: Can I turn suggestions into a different item type?
**A:** Suggestions become regular roadmap items when approved. They're the same post type, just with a "Pending" status initially.
