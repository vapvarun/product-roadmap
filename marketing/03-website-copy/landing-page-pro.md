**A:** No. It's a one-time purchase with lifetime updates. Pay once, use forever.

### Q: What happens when my license expires?
**A:** You can continue using all Pro features. You just won't receive updates or priority support. Renew anytime to restore them.

### Q: Can I upgrade from a single site to unlimited?
**A:** Yes. Contact support and we'll apply a prorated upgrade.

### Q: Do I lose data if I start with Free and upgrade later?
**A:** No. Upgrading is seamless. Install Pro alongside Free and your existing data stays intact.

### Q: Is there a trial version?
**A:** We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee instead. Try Pro with full functionality and get a refund if it's not right for you.

### Q: What support is included?
**A:** Priority email support. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.

### Q: Can I use Pro on client sites?
