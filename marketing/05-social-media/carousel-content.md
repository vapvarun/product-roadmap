## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### LinkedIn Carousel
- **Size:** 1080 x 1080 px (square) or 1080 x 1350 px (portrait)
- **Format:** PDF or multiple images
- **Max slides:** 20 (aim for 6-10)
- **File size:** Under 100MB

### Instagram Carousel
- **Size:** 1080 x 1080 px (square) recommended
- **Format:** Multiple images
- **Max slides:** 10
- **Text:** Minimal, readable on mobile

### Design Tips
- Consistent style across slides
- Bold, readable fonts (min 24pt equivalent)
- High contrast for accessibility
- Brand colors throughout
- First slide is the hook - make it count
- Last slide has clear CTA

### Brand Elements
- WBcom Designs logo (small, corner)
- Consistent color palette
- Product Roadmap name/icon where relevant
- Website URL on CTA slides
