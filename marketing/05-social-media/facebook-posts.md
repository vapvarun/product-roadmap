## POST 2: Educational - Why Roadmaps Matter

**Post:**

Why does every SaaS seem to have a public roadmap these days?

Because it works.

Here's what happens when you share your product plans publicly:

**1. Customer trust increases**
They see you're working on things, not just collecting money.

**2. Feedback gets organized**
Instead of scattered requests, everything's in one place.

**3. Prioritization gets easier**
Votes tell you what customers actually want.

**4. Churn decreases**
Customers about to leave often stay when they see their requested feature is "In Progress."

**5. Community forms**
People discussing features become invested in your success.

---

The objection I hear most: "But we can't share our roadmap!"

Counterpoint: Linear, Notion, and hundreds of successful companies do.

Your competitors can already see your released features. A roadmap just shows what's coming.

The upside (trust, feedback, engagement) outweighs the risk.

## POST 3: Feature Highlight - Voting

**Post:**

Quick product tip: If you're collecting feature requests, let customers VOTE on them.

Here's why:

**Without voting:**
You have a list of requests. No idea which ones matter most. Whoever is loudest wins.

**With voting:**
You have a ranked list. Real data showing what customers actually want. Evidence for decisions.

---

The key: Make voting EASY.

Guest voting (no account required) = 10x more votes
Quick single click = more participation
Instant feedback = satisfying experience

---

If you're on WordPress, Product Roadmap includes full voting for free.

But whatever tool you use, voting transforms how you collect feedback.

Stop guessing. Start counting.

### Group Rules
- Always check group rules before posting

- Max 1-2 group posts per week total

## GENERAL FACEBOOK GUIDELINES

### Image Recommendations
- 1200 x 630 pixels optimal
- Product screenshots with context
- Avoid text-heavy images
- Use branded templates

### Video
- Square (1:1) or vertical (4:5) format
- Caption for sound-off viewing
- Under 3 minutes for engagement
- Hook in first 3 seconds

### Best Times
- 1-4 PM on weekdays
- 12-1 PM on weekends
- Test your specific audience

### Engagement
- Reply to all comments
- Ask questions in posts
- Use polls for engagement
- Thank people for sharing
