# Introduction to Product Roadmap

Product Roadmap is a WordPress plugin that helps you share your product plans publicly, collect feedback from users, and let them vote on the features that matter most to them.

Instead of losing feature requests in support tickets, emails, and one-off conversations, Product Roadmap gives you a single public page where customers can see what's planned, vote on what they want, comment on items, and submit their own ideas.

## What you can do

**As the site owner:**

- Create and organize roadmap items with statuses, categories, and priorities
- Display your roadmap on any page using a shortcode
- Collect votes to help prioritize what to build next
- Let visitors comment and discuss individual items
- Accept feature suggestions from members, with a moderation queue
- Publish changelogs documenting what shipped in each release
- Track progress with a percentage indicator per item

**As a visitor or member:**

- Vote on the features they want (guest or logged-in, depending on your settings)
- Comment on roadmap items
- Submit their own feature suggestions
- See what's planned, in progress, and completed
- Read your changelog to see what's new
