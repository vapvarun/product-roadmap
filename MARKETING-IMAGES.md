## 🎨 Design Guidelines

### Colors
```
Primary Blue: #2271b1
Dark Gray: #50575e
Success Green: #46b450
Pro Purple: #667eea
Warning Gold: #f0b849
Light Background: #f0f0f1
White: #ffffff
```

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Inter Bold or Poppins Bold
- **Body Text**: Inter Regular or Open Sans
- **Button Text**: Inter Medium
- **Code/Technical**: JetBrains Mono

### Visual Elements
- Use rounded corners (8-12px radius)
- Subtle shadows for depth
- Clean, minimal icons
- Plenty of white space
- WordPress admin UI elements for familiarity

### Brand Personality
- Professional but approachable
- Modern and clean
- Trustworthy and reliable
- Developer-friendly
- Community-focused
